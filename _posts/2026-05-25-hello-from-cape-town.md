This is the first post on my new self-hosted blog. The plan is to write here whenever I land somewhere new or notice something worth keeping.

## Why I'm here

I'm two weeks into the **Cooperative AI Research Fellowship** in Cape Town. The cohort is twelve people from eleven countries, picked from about 1,100 applicants. Most of us are working on some flavor of multi-agent AI systems — how groups of LLM-based agents coordinate, fail, and recover.

## Early impressions

A few things I didn't expect:

1. **The tech community here is doing genuinely interesting work** at the intersection of AI and global development — not as a side concern, but as the central question.
2. **The pace is different.** Slower in the best way. Conversations are longer, decisions feel more deliberate.
3. **The food.** I'll write a separate post.

## What's next

I'm spending the next few weeks getting my prototype off the ground. After that, I want to take a long weekend on the Western Cape coast — Cape Point, the Cederberg, maybe Hermanus for whale-watching season.

More soon.
